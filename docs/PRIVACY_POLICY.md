## Accounts
You need an account to use Habitrail. What we process for it is listed below.

## Information we collect when you create an account
Accounts are handled through Supabase. When you sign up or sign in we process:
- **Account details** — your email address, a password (stored hashed by
  Supabase; we never see it in plain text), your username and display name.
- **Progress snapshot** — your level/XP, current streak, prestige, and chosen
  profile border. This is shown to other signed-in users on friend lists and
  leaderboards.
- **Social data** — your friend connections, the parties and guilds you join,
  and the **chat messages** you send. Party and guild messages are visible to
  members of that group; **global chat messages are visible to all signed-in
  users**. Don't post anything you wouldn't want others to see.
- **Cloud backup (optional)** — if you're signed in, a copy of your on-device
  save (habits, journal entries, food log, milestones, tasks, settings) is
  synced to your account so you can restore it on a new device. Only you can
  read your own backup.

## Information processed for AI features
To rate a new habit's difficulty and to power the AI coach, weekly plans, emoji
suggestions, and nutrition estimates, text you enter (such as a habit's name)
is sent to our own AI service and processed on our infrastructure. It is **not**
used to train third-party models or sold to anyone. If the service is
unavailable, the app falls back to on-device logic.

## Information stored only on your device
Your habits, journal entries, food log, focus sessions, app settings, and your
profile photo (picked by you and downscaled) are stored locally on your device
and are included in a cloud backup only if you are signed in.

## Payments
Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription purchased through Apple's
In-App Purchase. **Apple** processes the payment; we do not receive or store
your card or payment details. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID
settings.

## What we do NOT do
- No third-party advertising.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs; we do not track you across other
  apps or websites.
- We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.

## How your data is protected
Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Server-side access is restricted with
Supabase Row-Level Security so you can only read and write your own records
(aside from the public progress snapshot and chat described above).

## Deleting your account and data
You can delete your account at any time from **Account → Delete account**. This
permanently removes your account and the data linked to it (profile, friends,
party/guild membership, messages, and cloud backup). Data stored only on your
device is removed when you delete the app.

## Children
Habitrail is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has
provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have the right to access,
correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these
in-app (edit your profile, export via backup, delete your account) or by
contacting us at **habitrailapp@gmail.com**.
